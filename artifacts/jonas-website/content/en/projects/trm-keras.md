## Context

The paper “Less is More: Recursive Reasoning with Tiny Networks” shows that a very small network can solve hard reasoning tasks by applying the same layers repeatedly. This is exactly the direction that interests me: fewer parameters, more recursion.

## Contribution

I rebuilt the architecture from the paper myself in Keras to understand the structure of the recursive loop and the training in detail.
